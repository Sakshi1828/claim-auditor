Redesign · Phase 1 of 8

New look: what we checked and what we changed

This is the first step of making the Claim Auditor easier on the eyes and easier to understand. In this step we only looked, measured, and changed the colours and letters. Nothing the app does was changed.

Opens your print window: choose “Save as PDF”.
✓ Everything still works exactly as before. We checked all 24 test claims, every button and every download, and all 5 test packs passed.

1. What the app is made of

The app is one web page, written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no extra add-ons). The checking is done by Python on your computer. There is no chart add-on: the few bars you see are drawn by the page itself.

3. Where the numbers come from

5. Where the page is hardest to read today

  1. The claim details panel (right side of Step 2) is long: many boxes stacked on top of each other.
  2. “How this claim was analysed” is a table of technical words (field names, steps).
  3. Step 3 has two long lists side by side (what to do, what it can't tell you).
  4. The guide strip sentence is sometimes long and wraps onto 3 lines on small screens.
  5. Field names such as PAM50Call_RNAseq appear in places a normal reader doesn't need them.

6. Our “before” record (to prove nothing changes later)

We saved a record of everything the app shows for two test reports: 24 claims, every verdict, every number, every next step, every plain-words sentence, and the text of every downloaded file, plus all 17 buttons and 67 page parts. After today's change we compared again: no difference at all. We will repeat this check at the end of every phase.

8. Letters and spacing

Headings: an elegant book-style font (like this). Normal text: a clear, modern font, slightly larger than before (15.5 instead of 15) with a little more space between lines. We used fonts that are already on the computer, so the app still works without internet and sends nothing out.

We also set fixed sizes for gaps, rounded corners and soft shadows, so every later screen looks consistent.

11. What changed and what did not

Changed: colours, fonts, text size and line spacing; the small tab icon colour; a hidden picture library.

Not changed: any button, box, wording, number, result, download, calculation, or anything behind the screen. Nothing was risky, so nothing had to be skipped.
