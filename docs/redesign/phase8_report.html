What we polished

Fits every screen size

TestedPhone (375 wide), tablet (768), small laptop (1024) and large screen (1440). On each one we loaded a report, opened every tab and the side panel, and checked that nothing spills off the side. Nothing did.
FixedOn phones and tablets every button, link and switch is now at least 44 pixels tall, a comfortable size for a finger. This covers the step links at the top, fold-outs, small chart dots and the dotted help words.
FixedIn 'Read the report with its sources', lines are spaced further apart on touch screens so each sentence is easy to tap.

Easy to read for everyone

FixedThe small group numbers were white on light colours, which was hard to read. They are now dark on white with a coloured ring (about 14 to 1 contrast; the rule asks for 4.5 to 1).
TestedText, labels, status pills and help bubbles all pass the standard contrast rule (WCAG AA) in light and dark mode.
TestedEvery control shows a clear outline when reached with the keyboard. Headings go in order (page title, then sections, then parts). Buttons, charts and the side panel have spoken labels for screen readers.

Gentle motion

TestedMovement is short and soft (0.15 to 0.3 seconds). If a person's computer asks for less motion, the animations switch off.

Light and fast

TestedNo photos, no outside fonts and no outside chart library. All drawings are small built-in line drawings, so the page loads quickly and works without internet.
UnchangedOnce results appear, the welcome banner hides to make room. This was a planned change from Phase 3, not a jump.

How we proved nothing broke

We actually ran everything, we did not assume:

  1. The app was opened and used. We loaded the practice report and the demo report and opened every result, every tab and all three views (Lead, Reviewer, Researcher).
  2. No errors. The browser showed no error messages.
  3. Every button and form. Confirm, Override (with a reason), the final decision, and Export all worked. The organizers' checker said the export is valid.
  4. Same words. All 574 pieces of text from before the redesign are still on the page, word for word.
  5. Same results. A side-by-side comparison with the original app (the main version) found every result, button and export identical. Nothing was removed or edited.
  6. Tests. All 5 test packs pass.
