# Clinical characteristics of the TCGA-BRCA cohort (test report)

Infiltrating ductal carcinoma accounts for about 72% of tumors.
About 18% of tumors are infiltrating lobular carcinoma.
Lobular carcinoma makes up 25% of tumors with a recorded histology.
Stage II tumors make up about 58% of cases with a recorded stage.
Roughly 1 in 4 tumors were stage III or higher.
Stage IV disease was present in 5% of tumors.
About half of patients had lymph node involvement.
Most tumors were node-negative.
Fewer than 3% of patients had distant metastasis at diagnosis.
Nearly all patients in the cohort are female.
Most patients were postmenopausal at diagnosis.
Positive surgical margins were found in 12% of tumors.
More than half of patients received radiation therapy.
