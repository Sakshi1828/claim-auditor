# Demo test report: breast cancer cohort summary

TCGA-BRCA is a valuable resource for breast cancer research.

Luminal A accounts for about 50% of primary tumors with an RNA subtype call. Basal-like tumors make up 25% of primary tumors with an RNA subtype call.

TP53 is mutated in about a third of primary tumors with mutation data. PIK3CA is mutated in 60% of ER-positive tumors. ERBB2 is amplified in about 12% of tumors with copy number data.

The median age at diagnosis was 65 years. Patients older than 65 make up about 29% of the cohort. About 14% of patients with survival data died.

Stage IIA accounts for about a third of tumors with a recorded stage. Fewer than 800 primary tumors have protein data. Of the 1,101 primary tumors in the release, 535 have survival, mutation, copy number, RNA and protein data.

ER-alpha protein is higher in ER-positive tumors than in ER-negative tumors.

The median number of lymph nodes examined was 9. Lumpectomy was performed in about 23% of patients.

Tamoxifen works best in Luminal A tumors. Grade 3 tumors make up 40% of the cohort.

We recommend validating these findings in an independent cohort.
