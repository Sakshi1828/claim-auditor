# Held-out test (team-written, fictional)

*Written before running the tool on it. New phrasing and topics. Checked against TCGA-BRCA release A.*

Roughly two-thirds of primary tumors with a definite result are PR-positive.

HER2-enriched tumors make up under 10% of tumors with an RNA subtype call.

Basal-like is the third most common RNA subtype.

Luminal A is the most common RNA subtype.

CDH1 is mutated in 13% of primary tumors with mutation data.

At least 250 primary tumors carry a TP53 mutation.

TP53 mutations are found in fewer than 15% of luminal A tumors.

PIK3CA is mutated in about 45% of ER-positive tumors.

The release has 139 normal tissue samples and fewer than 10 metastatic samples.

Nearly all primary tumors have survival data.

About 2.8 slides are available per patient.

More than 1,100 slides are diagnostic slides.

Around 23% of patients are older than 65 at diagnosis.

Fewer than 5% of patients were diagnosed before age 35.

The mean age at diagnosis is about 58 years.

Over 150 deaths were recorded among primary tumors with survival data.

HER2 protein is lower in HER2-positive tumors.

GATA3 is mutated more often than MAP3K1.

Luminal A and Luminal B together account for about 70% of tumors with an RNA subtype call.

Copy number data is available for over 1,000 primary tumors.
