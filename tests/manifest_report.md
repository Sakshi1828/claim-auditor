# Manifest stress test (written by the team, fictional)

The slide manifest lists 3,111 slides, of which 1,133 are diagnostic and 1,978 are frozen sections.

The sample manifest accounts for 3,111 slides through slides_for_sample.

1,098 of the 1,101 primary tumors have at least one slide.

1,062 patients have a diagnostic slide.

3 primary tumors have no slides.

The median slide is about 338 MB.

All slides together take up 1.7 TB.

About 83% of slides were scanned at 40x.

All slides are released under CC BY 3.0.

Each patient has 3.5 slides on average.

Every slide has a viewer link.

The sample manifest lists 1,247 samples from 1,101 patients, including 139 normal tissue samples and 7 metastatic samples.

Most slides were scanned at 20x.
