# Routing regression report

MYC is amplified in about 21% of tumors with copy number data.
ERBB2 is amplified in 25% of tumors.
CCND1 amplification occurs in roughly 16% of tumors.
CDKN2A is deeply deleted in 4.4% of tumors.
MYC is amplified in nearly half of basal-like tumors.
About 13% of patients with survival data had disease progression.
Recurrence was recorded in 20% of patients.
About 8% of patients died of breast cancer.
HER2 protein is higher in Luminal A tumors than in Basal-like tumors.
HER2 protein is higher in Basal-like tumors than in HER2-enriched tumors.
Stage IIA accounts for about a third of tumors with a recorded stage.
Basal tumors make up 21.6% of the RPPA clusters.
97% of patients had no distant metastasis at diagnosis.
32.4% of tumors with a recorded AJCC stage (2012 study) are Stage IIA.
