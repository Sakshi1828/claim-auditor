# Stress test report (written by the team, fictional)

The release covers 1,101 patients and 1,247 samples, including 139 normal tissue samples and 7 metastatic samples.

About 77% of primary tumors with a definite result were ER-positive.

HER2-positive tumors make up 35% of primary tumors with a definite IHC result.

Basal-like tumors account for 16.7% of primary tumors with an RNA subtype call, and HER2-enriched tumors for 7.9%.

TP53 is the most frequently mutated gene, mutated in 45% of primary tumors with mutation data.

PIK3CA is mutated in about 33% of primary tumors with mutation data.

Of the 3,111 slides, 1,978 are frozen sections and 1,133 are diagnostic slides.

The median age at diagnosis was 58 years.

ER-alpha protein is lower in ER-negative tumors than in ER-positive tumors.

HER2 protein is higher in HER2-positive tumors.

612 primary tumors have both RNA and protein data.

Triple-negative tumors are about 18% of primary tumors with all three receptor results.

Basal-like tumors have worse survival than Luminal A tumors.

Tamoxifen works best in Luminal A tumors.

In the 2012 Nature subtype calls, Luminal A accounts for 44.3% of subtyped primary tumors.
